Impact across my portfolio

Device health & operational outcomes

  • Improved aggregate device health across my portfolio from 89% to 91%, driven primarily by turnarounds at Cassens Transport, NexTier, and UNFI.
  • At Cassens, improved overall device health from 88% to 91.1% and raised AG health to target levels through focused execution on gateway issues and unhealthy assets.
  • Resolved a roughly 49-million-mile odometer discrepancy at Cassens by identifying unplugged gateways as the root cause, implementing Gateway Unplugged Alerts, and restoring confidence in downstream maintenance data.

Support efficiency & self-sufficiency

  • Reduced how-to tickets from 32% to 26% by moving customers toward greater self-sufficiency and creating more room for higher-value technical work.
  • Generated the highest number of customer survey responses on the team in the February CSAT cycle.

Commercial impact

  • Supported the UNFI account team in closing a $340K upsell for Connected Training and Commercial Navigation, and was recognized in the associated win motion.
  • Helped create broader expansion momentum at UNFI across AI Multicam, Connected Workflows, Forklift Safety, and Asset Tracking by tying technical adoption and account strategy together.
  • At UNFI, stepped in at a critical moment to stabilize active support issues and establish a strong operating cadence through weekly syncs and iterative technical reviews, tightening alignment between the customer, Support, and the account team. This sustained Q4/Q1 expansion momentum, kept device health moving, helped position UNFI's Chief Safety Officer for the Beyond mainstage, and put the account on track from Yellow to Green sentiment.
  • At Cassens, turned a customer operational pain point into a tracker upsell conversation by identifying an AT11/AT12 use case to reduce hardware loss in transit.

Strategic customer ownership

  • Drove a critical cross-functional escalation for BrandSafway on device activity and total on-time API data, coordinating across legal, technical, and account stakeholders to keep a red-sentiment strategic account moving toward resolution.
  • Owned high-severity escalations across PFG and APi Group, including analysis of drowsiness model behavior and root-cause determination on a missing crash video caused by loss of camera power at impact.

Scalable TAM impact

Technical Health Review

  • Drove the Technical Health Review, a strategic FY27 priority designed to give customers an executive-level view of fleet health across five pillars.
  • Built the framework, delivered the first pilots, and established the foundation for a repeatable deliverable that can scale across the TAM organization.

TAM Command Center & AI workflow innovation

  • Built the TAM Command Center to provide a single view of tasks, customer context, and outcomes across Gmail, Slack, Calendar, Jira, Zendesk, and Gong, reducing manual prep and making account prioritization easier.
  • Presented it to the full team, where it was recognized as a practical way to reduce TAM overhead and improve execution quality.
  • Approved as a RevUp capstone and later showcased in Support AI Bootcamp as a concrete example of AI improving TAM workflows.

Reusable tooling & process improvement

  • Built the Gateway Deactivation script and related workflow improvements, later incorporated into an internal Support Tools resource for broader use across the organization.
  • The solution supported batch deactivation through the public API with dry-run preview, region support, and reporting, and has already been used across multiple accounts to clean up dormant hardware and improve asset gateway health.
